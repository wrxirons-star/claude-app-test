# EXCESS FUNDS RECOVERY ASSISTANCE AGREEMENT

State of Georgia | {county} County
Tax sale reference: {case_number}
Property: {property_address} (Parcel {parcel_id})
Date: {agreement_date_long}

This Agreement is between **{claimant_name}** ("Claimant"), {claimant_address}, and **{business_name}** ("Recovery Agent"), {business_address}.

## 1. Background
The property was sold at a tax sale on {sale_date_long}. The {holder} is holding excess funds of approximately **{surplus_display}** in escrow, first placed in escrow on or about {deposit_date_long}. The Claimant was the record owner, or holds a recorded interest in the property, as of the date of the tax sale.

## 2. Claimant's right to claim without assistance
The Claimant understands that the Claimant may file a claim for the excess funds directly with the {holder} at no charge, and that the Claimant has been told this in writing before signing.

## 3. Timing
The parties acknowledge that under Georgia law an agreement of this kind is unenforceable for 24 months after the funds were first placed in escrow. This Agreement is dated on or after {earliest_agreement_long}, which is after that period.

## 4. Services
The Recovery Agent will prepare the county excess funds claim package, gather proof of ownership and identity, arrange notarization, submit the claim (or, where the county requires it, deliver the package for the Claimant or the Claimant's attorney to submit), and follow up until the funds are paid or the claim is denied.

## 5. Fee
The Claimant agrees to pay the Recovery Agent a fee of **{fee_percent} of the excess funds actually received by the Claimant**, estimated at **{fee_display}**, which does not exceed 10 percent of the value of the excess being recovered. No fee is owed if nothing is received.

## 6. Payment goes to the Claimant
All excess funds will be paid by the {holder} directly to the Claimant. The Recovery Agent will not receive the funds, will not hold a power of attorney to receive them, and will invoice the Claimant for the fee within 10 days after the Claimant receives payment. The invoice is due 15 days after it is sent.

## 7. Cancellation
The Claimant may cancel by written notice within 7 days after signing, or at any time before the claim is filed, at no charge.

## 8. No legal advice; attorney filings
The Recovery Agent is not a law firm and does not give legal advice. If the {holder} accepts filings only from the Claimant or a Georgia attorney, the Recovery Agent will prepare the package for that filing. {attorney_sentence}

## 9. Entire agreement
This is the whole agreement. Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties. Georgia law governs.
