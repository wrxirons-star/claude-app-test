## 1. Background
On {sale_date_long} the property was sold at a {sale_kind}. The sale produced surplus funds now held by the {holder}. The amount held, according to the {holder}'s records as of {today_long}, is **{surplus_display}** (the "Surplus"). The Owner was the owner of record of the property at the time of the filing of the lis pendens or the tax deed application.

## 2. Owner's right to claim without assistance
The Owner understands that the Owner may claim the Surplus directly from the {holder} at no charge, without this Agreement or the Recovery Agent, and that the Owner has been told this in writing before signing.

## 3. Services
The Recovery Agent will prepare the claim, gather supporting documents, arrange notarization, submit the claim to the {holder} or the court, respond to requests for information, and keep the Owner informed until the Surplus is disbursed or the claim is denied.

## 4. Compensation and partial assignment
As the Recovery Agent's only compensation, the Owner assigns to the Recovery Agent **{fee_percent} of the Surplus actually disbursed** on the Owner's claim, which the Owner and the Recovery Agent estimate to be **{fee_display}** based on the amount held today. The Owner retains the remaining {owner_percent}. The Recovery Agent's total compensation, whether paid or payable, earned or expected, will not exceed 12 percent of the Surplus (section 45.033(3), Florida Statutes). The Owner owes nothing if no Surplus is disbursed.

## 5. Disclosures
- Total surplus held: {surplus_display}

## 6. Cancellation
The Owner may cancel this Agreement by written notice within 7 days after signing, or at any time before the claim is filed, without any charge.

## 7. Filing
The Owner agrees that a copy of this Agreement may be filed with the court and the {holder} so the court can honor the assignment and direct the disbursement.

## 8. No legal advice
The Recovery Agent is not a law firm and does not give legal advice. The Owner may consult an attorney before signing.

## 9. Entire agreement
This is the whole agreement. Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties. Florida law governs.
