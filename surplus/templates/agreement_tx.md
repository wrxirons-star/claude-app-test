## 1. Background
The property was sold at a tax foreclosure sale on {sale_date_long}. Excess proceeds of **{surplus_display}** were deposited in the registry of the court on or about {deposit_date_long}. The Assignor was the owner of the property at the time of the sale and holds a claim to the excess proceeds under section 34.04, Texas Tax Code.

## 2. Assignor's rights without this assignment
The Assignor understands that the Assignor may petition the court for the excess proceeds directly, at no charge other than court costs, before {petition_deadline_long}, or may hire an attorney whose fee is limited by law to the lesser of 25 percent or $1,000. The Assignor has been told this in writing.

## 3. Payment to Assignor
On the date of this assignment the Assignee has paid the Assignor **{purchase_display}**, which is **{purchase_percent}** of the Assignor's claim of {surplus_display} and not less than 80 percent of that claim. Receipt is acknowledged by the Assignor's signature below. Method of payment: ______________________.

## 4. Assignment
For that payment, the Assignor assigns and transfers to the Assignee all of the Assignor's right, title, and interest in the claim to the excess proceeds in the above cause. The Assignor understands that the court may not order more than 125 percent of the amount paid to the Assignor (that is, not more than {max_recovery_display}) to be paid to the Assignee.

## 5. Statutory representations
The Assignor and the Assignee each state that:
- this assignment is taken on or after the 36th day after the date the excess proceeds were deposited in the registry of the court;
- this assignment is in writing and signed by the Assignor;
- this assignment is **not** the result of an in-person or telephone solicitation; contact was made only by mail or email;
- no fee has been or will be charged by the Assignee to the Assignor.

## 6. Cooperation
The Assignor will sign any additional affidavit or document the court or district clerk reasonably requires to confirm the Assignor's identity and ownership at the time of sale.

## 7. No legal advice
The Assignee is not a law firm and does not give legal advice. The Assignor may consult an attorney before signing.
